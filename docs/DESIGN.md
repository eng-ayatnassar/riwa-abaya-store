## Brand & Style

This design system expresses quiet Gulf luxury through the lens of bespoke couture abayas and refined modest tailoring. The brand voice balances architectural precision with soft, fluid elegance, evoking an intimate atelier environment. Designed for an international, culturally discerning clientele, the aesthetic is editorial, serene, and deliberate.

The interface embraces high-end editorial minimalism: generous negative space, deliberate proportions, delicate 1px borderlines resembling couture seams, and quiet metallic accents. Layouts prioritize art direction, portrait photography, and bilingual balance, avoiding visual clutter to allow fabrics, drapes, and craftsmanship to dominate the visual field.

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration from raw silk, burnished desert sunlight, and deep onyx pigments. The interface avoids cold starkness in favor of luminous, tactile warmth.

- **Primary (`#C5A880` - Burnished Champagne):** Applied with disciplined restraint to key interactive focus states, thin callouts, metallic accents, and bespoke iconography. Never used in excessive solid blocks.
- **Secondary (`#171615` - Onyx Charcoal):** The anchor for high-contrast typography, primary editorial CTAs, and structural accents. Softened slightly from pure black to avoid clinical hardness.
- **Tertiary (`#D9C7B0` - Warm Sand):** A supportive metallic tint used for subtle badge fills, quiet hovers, and secondary active indicators.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FAF8F5` - Silk Alabaster):** The luminous base layer providing editorial warmth.
- **Surface & Framing:** Container layers sit on `#FFFFFF` (Pure Silk), surrounded by `#EFECE6` (Pearl Seam) hairline dividers and `#F4F1EB` (Oyster Linen) contextual surfaces.
- **Body & Secondary Copy (`#4A4845` - Slate Charcoal):** Softened dark tone ensuring optimal reading contrast without visual heaviness.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy creates a dialogue between haute couture heritage and modern, effortless clarity.

- **Headlines & Editorial Titles:** Set in `Playfair Display`. The high-contrast letterforms capture classical luxury, grace, and haute couture gravitas. Used for lookbook titles, collection manifests, and hero statements.
- **Body & Technical Specs:** Set in `Plus Jakarta Sans`. Its geometric yet humane rhythm provides effortless legibility across garment compositions, measurements, atelier storytelling, and checkout flows.
- **Uppercase Labels:** Set in `Plus Jakarta Sans` with expanded letter-spacing (`0.08em` to `0.12em`) for subheadings, category browses, and product states.
- **Bilingual & Arabic Adaptations:** Arabic headlines employ classical high-contrast calligraphic balance, while body copy maintains matching x-height and optical weight alignment with the sans-serif scale.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow an intentional editorial structure that mirrors archival publications and luxury lookbooks.

- **Grid Architecture:** Desktop views use a 12-column layout with 2.5rem (`40px`) gutters and 4rem (`64px`) page margins, framing content with generous negative space. Mobile views condense to a 4-column layout with 1.5rem (`24px`) gutters and 1.25rem (`20px`) margins.
- **Asymmetric Tension:** Imagery and editorial text frequently alternate in asymmetric offsets (e.g., 7-column feature visual paired with a 4-column atelier story) to evoke curated print journals.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Macro-sections utilize generous spacing (`space-xl` and extended multiples) to separate garments and collections, preventing cognitive overload and conveying exclusivity.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy shadows, drop glows, and layered blur gimmicks. Depth is established through planar layering, hairline framing, and tactile transparency.

- **Hairline Framing:** Surface separation is achieved primarily through crisp 1px borders in `#EFECE6` (Pearl) and `#C5A880` (Champagne).
- **Subtle Surface Tiers:** Elevating content from canvas (`#FAF8F5`) to active card or panel surfaces relies on solid `#FFFFFF` or softened `#F4F1EB` overlays.
- **Ambient Atelier Shadow:** Reserved exclusively for floating sheets, bespoke drawer overlays, and dropdown menus. The shadow is ultra-diffused, using warm charcoal with an amber tint: `0 20px 40px -10px rgba(23, 22, 21, 0.05), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(23, 22, 21, 0.02)`.
- **Modals & Drawers:** Accompanied by a semi-sheer alabaster backdrop (`rgba(250, 248, 245, 0.75)`) combined with a soft blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`) reflecting silk organza layering.

## Shapes

The shape system is tailored and architectural, reflecting the clean seams and structure of haute abayas.

- **Base Radius (Roundedness 1 - `0.25rem` / `4px`):** Used on interactive inputs, primary action buttons, content cards, and image viewports to balance precision with warmth.
- **Accent Elements (`rounded-lg` / `8px`):** Subtle curve for dropdown menus, toasts, and floating quick-view overlays.
- **Pill Selectors:** Specialized sizing chips and bilingual toggle capsules utilize fully rounded pill silhouettes (`9999px`) to create an organic, tactile contrast against rectilinear editorial grids.

## Components

### Buttons & Editorial Actions
- **Primary Button:** `#171615` (Onyx) background, `#FAF8F5` typography, 1px border matching the background. Hover shifts gracefully to `#2A2826` with subtle letter-spacing expansion. Padding: `12px 28px`.
- **Secondary / Atelier Button:** Ghost style with `#FAF8F5` surface, 1px `#C5A880` border, `#171615` typography. Hover transitions to `#FAF8F5` with a `#D9C7B0` background wash.
- **Text Link:** Set in `label-md` uppercase, underlined with a 1px `#C5A880` rule positioned 4px below text baseline.

### Floating Badges & Pill Size Selectors
- **Floating Badges:** Ultra-minimal pill badges (`padding: 4px 10px`, `label-sm`) with a pure white background, 1px `#EFECE6` border, and `#171615` text, indicating editions (e.g., "BESPOKE", "LIMITED RUN").
- **Size & Length Chips:** Pill form factor (`36px` height, min-width `44px`). Default state features `#FAF8F5` background with 1px `#EFECE6` border. Active state shifts to `#171615` fill with `#FFFFFF` text. Disabled state shows a subtle diagonal slash with muted slate color.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Input Fields:** Flat baseline or minimal box styling with `#FFFFFF` surface, 1px `#EFECE6` border, and `4px` border radius. Focus state transitions the border to `#C5A880` without harsh focus rings. Floating labels transition using `label-sm`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Selection Controls:** Custom compact controls (`18px`). Checkboxes feature a thin 1px `#D9C7B0` border and fill with `#171615` when selected, sporting a warm champagne check icon. Radio buttons reveal a centered champagne dot.

### Editorial Product Cards
- **Card Framing:** Seamless `#FFFFFF` container with no shadow. Imagery uses a 4:5 editorial portrait ratio. Hover reveals secondary drape angles with a 600ms ease-out opacity transition.
- **Divider Rules:** Hairline 1px dividers in `#EFECE6` separate product metadata, keeping price and silhouette details clear.

### Bespoke Drawers & Accordions
- **Fabric & Detail Accordions:** Borderless rows separated by 1px `#EFECE6` dividers. Titles set in `title-md`, expanding smoothly to disclose silk origins, embroidery details, and care guides.
- **Atelier Drawer Overlay:** Sliding modal from the end edge for shopping bags and sizing appointments, utilizing an oyster linen canvas (`#F4F1EB`) and warm blur scrim.
